Chess Lessons

Chess lessons are taught in groups, with attention to everyone’s level and pace. Below you’ll find all the practical information.

Who is it for?

For children, teenagers and adults: from beginners to advanced players, everyone is welcome.

Structure?

We work in series of 10 lessons, with a clear structure and personal follow-up for every participant.

Duration?

Lessons last 1 hour per week. We start in October.

Location?

Lessons take place in a hall in Bertem.

Price?

€200 for 10 lessons for the first 24 participants, €220 after that.
