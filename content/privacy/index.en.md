## 1. Who are we?

This website is operated by **Grow Through Chess** ("we", "us"), located at Diestsesteenweg 49, 3010 Leuven, Belgium.

For questions about this privacy policy or about the processing of your personal data, you can contact us at: [info@growthroughchess.com](mailto:info@growthroughchess.com).

## 2. What data do we collect?

When you sign up for one of our chess courses through our online form (Google Forms), we collect the following data:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number

When you send us a message through the contact form on our website, we collect your name, your email address and the content of your message.

## 3. Why do we process this data, and on what legal basis?

We use this data to:

- process and confirm your registration for a chess course;
- contact you with practical information about the course (location, time, changes, etc.);
- contact you, if necessary, with questions about your registration.

The legal basis for this processing is the **performance of a contract** (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): you sign up for a service we offer, and we need this data to provide that service.

Messages sent through the contact form are used only to answer your question. The legal basis for this is our **legitimate interest** in replying to questions you ask us (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 4. How long do we keep your data?

We keep your data for **1 year** after your registration or after the end of the course, unless you ask us to delete it earlier or a longer legal retention period applies (for example for accounting purposes, if applicable).

Messages sent through the contact form are kept as long as needed to handle your question, and at most **1 year**.

## 5. Do we share your data with third parties?

For the registration form we use **Google Forms** (part of Google Workspace/Google LLC). Google acts as a processor of your data on our behalf.

Google is an American company, which means data may be processed in the United States. This transfer is based on the **EU-US Data Privacy Framework**, an adequacy decision of the European Commission that ensures an appropriate level of protection for data transferred to the US.

For the contact form we use **Web3Forms** (web3forms.com). This service forwards your message to our email address and acts as a processor on our behalf. According to [Web3Forms' privacy policy](https://web3forms.com/privacy), the messages themselves are not stored by them.

We never sell your data to third parties and do not share it for commercial purposes.

## 6. Visiting our website

Our website itself does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising services. We do use the following external services:

- **Hosting:** the website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). On every visit, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in order to serve and secure the website.
- **Fonts:** fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Your IP address is sent to Google when this happens.
- **Embedded content:** the *Sign Up* and *Contact* pages show a Google form and a Google Maps map. When these load, Google may process data such as your IP address and set its own cookies, in accordance with [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Facebook:** the bottom of the website contains a link to our Facebook page. You only connect to Facebook if you click this link.

This processing is based on our **legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in offering a working, secure and user-friendly website.

## 7. What are your rights?

You have the right to:

- **access** the data we hold about you;
- request **correction** of inaccurate data;
- request **deletion** of your data;
- **object** to the processing of your data;
- receive your data in a portable format (**data portability**);
- **withdraw** any consent you have given at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [info@growthroughchess.com](mailto:info@growthroughchess.com). We handle your request as quickly as possible, and in any case within one month of receipt.

## 8. Right to complain

If you believe we are not processing your personal data correctly, you have the right to file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit, GBA):

Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit<br>
Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels<br>

## 9. Confidentiality and security

Everyone who has access to your data (for example teachers or staff of Grow Through Chess) treats it confidentially and uses it only for the purposes described above.

We also take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

## 10. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The most recent version can always be found on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.
